## Fixed

- Video reaches the browser over network paths that drop large packets, such as some TURN relays and VPNs, where it used to stay behind the loading spinner while the audio played. `webrtc_streamer()` lowers `aiortc`'s video RTP payload size from 1300 to 1150 bytes, which keeps the packets within the 1200 bytes browsers use. The `STREAMLIT_WEBRTC_MAX_VIDEO_RTP_PAYLOAD_SIZE` environment variable sets a different size, and `0` keeps `aiortc`'s own.
